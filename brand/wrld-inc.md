## Standard clauses (suggested, not legal advice)

- **Governing law.** Texas.
- **Payment terms.** Net 15 default for ongoing clients; Net 30 for new clients; 50%/50% for project work.
- **Limitation of liability.** Typically capped at 12 months of fees paid.
- **Confidentiality.** Mutual, standard. NDA optional for pre-proposal.
- **Data handling.** Aligned with vendor partners' compliance posture (document the flow-down).

> Always get a human attorney to review final clause language before signing. Claude can draft, not advise.

## Avoid

- Jargon stacking. "Notwithstanding the foregoing, heretofore..." — rewrite it.
- Unilateral clauses that contradict WRLD's **Foundational Integrity** value.
- Stock contract boilerplate that conflicts with existing MSA templates — always check existing templates first.
