# WRLD.AI — Business AI Assistant Brief

> Load `brand/CLAUDE.md` first.

**Product.** WRLD.AI — business AI assistants, Power Platform Copilot Agents, automations tailored per-client.
**Audience.** SMB operators, department leads, anyone whose day is full of repetitive workflows.
**Relationship to flagship.** Most ambitious current initiative. Voice stays on-brand but leans forward-looking.

## Tone dial

Warmth **High** · Formality **Medium** · Technicality **Low-Med** · Urgency **Medium**.

Make AI feel approachable and useful, not magical. Concrete examples beat abstract "intelligence".

## Color weighting

Monochromatic with `#00adee` as the preferred hover/focus accent — it reads slightly more "intelligent / signal" than `#007fee` and distinguishes WRLD.AI from the flagship. Sparing use of `#007fee` for secondary affordances. No `#EE9300` except on upgrade/subscription moments.

## Positioning

> "WRLD.AI is AI that fits *your* business — tailored agents, tuned by humans who know your operations. Built on OpenAI and Microsoft Copilot. Owned and operated by WRLD."

Emphasize **client-specific customization** — this is the core differentiator vs. "just use ChatGPT".

## Approved claims (conservative by default)

- ✅ "Agents built on OpenAI and Microsoft Copilot platforms."
- ✅ "Tuned on a client-by-client basis by the WRLD team."
- ✅ "Automates repetitive tasks and surfaces insights from your data."
- ✅ "Integrates with Microsoft 365 and the Power Platform."
- ⚠️ Claims about specific model names / versions — confirm with Ridgeway before publishing.
- ❌ Any claim of fully autonomous decision-making, "AGI", "sentient", "no human in the loop".
- ❌ Specific percentage productivity gains without a citable case study.

## Must-haves

- Concrete example workflows (not abstract promises): "draft client status updates from Outlook," "summarize weekly tickets from ClickUp," etc.
- Privacy / data handling language — where client data goes, who sees it, what's logged.
- A clear "how we'd start" path: discovery call → agent scoping → pilot → production.

## Avoid

- "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x" — overpromising.
- Sci-fi visual language (glowing brains, neural network diagrams).
- Any suggestion that WRLD.AI replaces the client's team.
