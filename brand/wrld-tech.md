# wrld.tech — Flagship Brief

> Load `brand/CLAUDE.md` first. This file overlays flagship-specific guidance.

**Entity.** WRLD Tech Co. (DBA of WRLD Inc.)
**Property.** wrld.tech — the primary website and company front door.
**Audience.** SMB owners and decision-makers (1–250 employees), primarily Dallas/Fort Worth and surrounding regions. Mix of technical and non-technical readers.
**Role in the system.** This is the anchor brand. Everything else references this.

## Tone dial

Marketing warmth **High** · Formality **Medium** · Technicality **Low** · Urgency **Low**.

Lead with partnership and outcomes, not product features. Speak to the business, not the stack.

## Color weighting

100% monochromatic static surfaces. Accent `#007fee` is the default hover/focus color for every CTA and the WRLD wordmark. `#EE9300` appears only on commerce moments (the `portal.wrld.tech` link, a store CTA). `#00adee` is the gradient companion during motion-heavy hero sequences.

## Messaging emphasis

- Lead with **Pillar 1 (Strategic Partnership)** and **Pillar 4 (Comprehensive one-stop)**.
- Secondary: Pillar 2 (Proactive protection) and Pillar 5 (Innovation with integrity).
- De-emphasize Pillar 3 (SMB-specialized) on the flagship page itself — it's implicit. Mention on "Who we serve" / sub-pages.

## Must-haves on any flagship-facing deliverable

- The primary tagline: "Your Strategic Partner in Technology and Business Growth."
- A path to the service branches (wrld.host, wrld.design, wrld.one, WRLD.AI, WRLD.Services, WRLD.Support).
- Contact: `ridge@wrld.tech` · HQ Dallas.
- Values referenced in copy, not just listed — show them in action.

## Avoid

- "IT guy", "tech support", "fix your computer", "call us when it breaks".
- Enterprise-coded language ("Fortune 500", "hyperscale") unless literally accurate.
- Hard sell CTAs. Prefer "Start a conversation" or "See if we're a fit" over "Buy now."
