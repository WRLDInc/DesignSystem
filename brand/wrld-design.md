# wrld.design — Design Services Brief

> Load `brand/CLAUDE.md` first.

**Property.** wrld.design — web design and development, brand work, creative direction.
**Audience.** SMBs needing a website or visual identity refresh; marketing leads; founders who "know their site is due for a rebuild".
**Relationship to flagship.** Creative service branch. Tone dial tilts warmer and more expressive than flagship.

## Tone dial

Warmth **High** · Formality **Medium** · Technicality **Low-Med** · Urgency **Low**.

Aesthetic-forward. Show work, don't over-describe it. Inspirational but still measured.

## Color weighting

Monochromatic base with slightly more permission for motion-driven accent sweeps — the three accent colors may appear in a single **motion** gradient (never static). Static surfaces remain monochromatic. Hover accent default: `#007fee`.

## Positioning

> "Design that ships. We don't hand off Figma files — we build the sites we design, and we build them to work."

Emphasis on the dev-capable design team — what separates WRLD.design from agencies that "design pretty but ship broken".

## Must-haves

- Case studies or representative work thumbnails.
- Clear process summary: discovery → design → build → launch → ongoing.
- Pricing orientation (even "starts at" is better than "contact us").
- Link to wrld.host for hosting tie-in (one-stop message).

## Avoid

- Abstract agency-speak ("we amplify brands", "we craft experiences").
- Stock photography in the showcase.
- Over-designed micro-animations that undermine the minimalist brand.
