Service overview

The Essential tier delivers comprehensive, fully managed IT built to keep Northbend Logistics running without interruption. Your environment is proactively monitored around the clock, kept patched and current, and protected by a managed, SOC-backed security stack. Your team gets responsive helpdesk access, managed identity and password security, Hexnode MDM across every Apple device, and network monitoring.

Layered on top, a dedicated DevOps retainer gives you consistent hands-on support for your development and deployment workflow. We take ownership of the day-to-day technology, with a clear coverage window, included monthly support time per device, and a single accountable partner.

Everything that follows details exactly what that looks like: the included core platform, your device inventory and monthly investment, the dedicated DevOps retainer, the managed cybersecurity stack, onboarding, and ongoing support.
